# Go font source provenance

The unmodified `Go-Regular.ttf`, `Go-Bold.ttf` and `LICENSE.txt` were copied from
the locally installed `golang.org/x/image@v0.30.0/font/gofont/ttfs/` distribution.
`LICENSE.txt` is the complete, unchanged distribution `README`, including the
Bigelow & Holmes copyright and licence. No font modification or subset was made.

The unit-panel manifest pins all three retained files. Its fresh export snapshots
the exact same bytes for an offline preview. Keep the full licence with any
distribution of these fonts. These are text assets, independently of the newly
authored unit-panel frame and role icons.
