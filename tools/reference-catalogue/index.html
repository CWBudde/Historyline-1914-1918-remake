What this study establishes

This catalogue records the desired appearance directly from RefDesign2. Repeated craters, forests, hills and compounds remain separate specimens. Their differences are useful references, rather than defects to remove.

The image clearly shows circular excavations and worn dirt tracks. It does not clearly show a continuous trench network or a broad mud biome. Cross-road white bars are labelled as entrance/checkpoint candidates; only visible water spans are treated as bridge examples. The bright red bottom boundary and white circular symbols are indexed as overlays, separately from terrain.

These are reference crops with surrounding scenery, not isolated sprites, seamless textures or finished replacement assets. Enlargements reveal existing pixels and add no resolution. The illustrative hex guide cannot recover the source painting’s original layers.
