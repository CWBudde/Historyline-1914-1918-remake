# PLAN — Historyline 1914-1918 remake

**Goal:** a complete, playable remake in Go/Ebitengine with newly authored art (2×, optional 4×), a fully
recreated UI and an optional original-rules profile.

**Art direction:** continuous painted illustration (see [art/README.md](art/README.md)). Keep the
original's gameplay IDs, map placement, connection topology and information layouts; never ship original art
or close reconstructions. Unit sprites and portraits follow their reference closely in composition at the
original size, in the painted style.
Terrain must remain reusable in different compatible tile arrangements. Match the reference's materials,
scale and lighting; do not achieve PULSE resemblance by substituting a painting of its exact lake outline.

**Rule sets:** `Classic` (the inherited Python behaviour), `Standard` (Classic with fixes), and the original
profile in `internal/dosrules`, which is the game's default (`-rules dos`); `Classic` and `Standard` stay
selectable with `-rules` and keep their tests. Keep them separate; never mix their mechanics.

This file lists open work only; finished work is in git. Reference: the split battle screen in
[docs/dos-screen.md](docs/dos-screen.md), packs, grade, light, unit dressing and stamps in
[docs/runtime-assets.md](docs/runtime-assets.md), terrain targets and prompts in
[art/direction.md](art/direction.md). Tick a box only with evidence you ran. Record decisions here, not their
history.

## Where things stand

- **Rules engine** (`internal/game`): hex map, terrain and seasons, movement (Dijkstra, movement types),
  three-state fog with remembered units/owners, combat with counter fire and ammo, containers (HQ, depots,
  factories), capture, HQ victory, end turn. Unit stats and attacks follow the original unit table
  (`internal/data/testdata/unitdat.json`).
- **Original rules** (`internal/dosrules`, `dosbind`, `dosplay`, `dosai`): combat, movement, containers,
  visibility, the phase change and the command cycle `Game` (validation, queued attacks, ordered events,
  save/resume, capture, sapper trenches, victory and score, energy with repair and production), bound to the
  shipped levels and the 72 original maps, with a computer side that plays every level to its end.
- **Front end** (`cmd/historyline`): menus around a battle, camera with zoom, independent UI scale and asset
  density, HiDPI and fullscreen. Under `-rules dos` the original's split screen
  ([docs/dos-screen.md](docs/dos-screen.md)); under `Classic`/`Standard` the single-map HUD with minimap,
  text info box, placeholder battle panel and banners.
- **Runtime art**: packs built by `cmd/hlasset` from `assets/index.json`; unit and terrain catalogs with
  context-aware composition (`internal/mapcompose`), graded and lit in the pack build, with scattered crowns
  and meadow props. Interface packs build, but the game does not use them yet.
- **AI** (`internal/ai`): greedy prototype through game commands and its own vision; plays all shipped levels.
- **Art**: masters and `prompt.md` in `art/<kind>/<family>/`, runtime PNGs in `assets/`, `art/index.json`
  generated by `just art-index`. All 48 unit families have sprites and 1×/2×/4× portraits, bound to all 75
  gameplay unit IDs. Review them in the art book (`just artbook`) and the comparison desk
  (`just gfx-game-review`), and the PULSE render against the reference with `just reference`.

#### Terrain look: order of work

**Decision:** the terrain does not yet reach the target look. The direction, the measured verdict, the
world-scale and value targets and the prompts are in [art/direction.md](art/direction.md); the evidence is
local in `build/review/look-audit/`. This section sets the order. Cells are named `rLcC` (line, column,
1-based) as in `art/reference/pulse/reference.json`, which lists the exemplar cells and multi-hex features
of every family. The goal: the PULSE map rendered with the runtime art (`just artbook`, chapter "PULSE map")
reads as the same painting as the local reference `RefDesign2.png`, without visible hex steps at 1×, 2× and
4×; the same foundations carry the winter maps and the 72 campaign maps.

Most of the flatness comes from the pack build, not from the paintings, so code comes first:
- Sources are squeezed into one 142×80 lattice cell, sampled bilinearly without a prefilter and averaged
  over two cosets.
- Every family bakes the canonical meadow into its tiles.

Stop adding enumerated baked contexts. Each one fixes the old sampling in place, and Look 1 replaces them.
